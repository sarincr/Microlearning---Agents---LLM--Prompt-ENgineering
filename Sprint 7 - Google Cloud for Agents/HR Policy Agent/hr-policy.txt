# HR Policy Document

## Cymbal Landscaping Pvt. Ltd.

### Employee Handbook & HR Policy Manual

---

# 1. Introduction

Welcome to Cymbal Landscaping Pvt. Ltd. This Human Resources Policy Manual establishes the rules, guidelines, benefits, responsibilities, and procedures applicable to all employees. The objective of this handbook is to ensure a productive, ethical, safe, and inclusive workplace while aligning employee conduct with organizational goals.

All employees are expected to read, understand, and comply with the policies outlined in this document. These policies may be updated periodically based on legal requirements, organizational needs, or operational changes.

---

# 2. Equal Employment Opportunity Policy

Cymbal Landscaping is committed to providing equal employment opportunities to all individuals regardless of race, religion, gender, age, disability, marital status, nationality, sexual orientation, or any other legally protected category.

Employment decisions including recruitment, promotion, compensation, training, and disciplinary actions shall be based solely on merit, qualifications, performance, and business requirements.

Discrimination, harassment, or retaliation in any form will not be tolerated.

---

# 3. Code of Conduct

Employees are expected to maintain professionalism, honesty, integrity, and mutual respect while performing their duties.

Employees must:

* Treat colleagues, clients, and vendors respectfully
* Follow company policies and legal regulations
* Protect confidential company information
* Avoid conflicts of interest
* Maintain ethical business practices
* Use company resources responsibly

The following behaviors are prohibited:

* Harassment or bullying
* Theft or fraud
* Workplace violence
* Substance abuse during work hours
* Misuse of company property
* Unauthorized disclosure of confidential information

Violations may result in disciplinary action including termination.

---

# 4. Employment Categories

Employees are classified as:

## Full-Time Employees

Employees working 40 hours per week on a permanent basis.

## Part-Time Employees

Employees working fewer than standard weekly hours.

## Contract Employees

Employees hired for specific projects or fixed durations.

## Temporary Employees

Employees hired for short-term operational requirements.

Eligibility for benefits may vary depending on employment category.

---

# 5. Working Hours and Attendance

Standard office hours are from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday, with a one-hour lunch break.

Employees are expected to:

* Report to work on time
* Record attendance accurately
* Notify managers regarding absences or delays
* Obtain approval for planned leave

Flexible working arrangements may be permitted based on managerial approval and operational requirements.

Excessive absenteeism, repeated tardiness, or attendance violations may lead to corrective action.

---

# 6. Remote Work Policy

Eligible employees may work remotely subject to managerial approval.

Employees working remotely must:

* Maintain availability during working hours
* Ensure internet connectivity and secure system access
* Protect company information and devices
* Attend scheduled meetings virtually

The company reserves the right to modify or revoke remote work privileges at any time.

---

# 7. Leave Policy

## Annual Leave

Full-time employees are entitled to 24 paid annual leave days per calendar year.

Unused leave up to 10 days may be carried forward to the next year.

## Sick Leave

Employees are entitled to 12 sick leave days annually.

Medical certificates may be required for absences exceeding 3 consecutive days.

## Casual Leave

Employees may utilize 6 casual leave days annually for personal matters.

## Maternity Leave

Eligible employees are entitled to maternity leave in accordance with applicable labor laws.

## Paternity Leave

Eligible employees may avail 5 working days of paid paternity leave.

## Bereavement Leave

Employees may receive up to 3 days of leave in the event of an immediate family member’s death.

All leave requests should be submitted through the company HR system for approval.

---

# 8. Compensation and Payroll

Employees are compensated according to their employment contracts and salary structures approved by management.

Salary payments are processed monthly through direct bank transfer.

Payroll deductions may include:

* Income tax
* Social security contributions
* Insurance premiums
* Other legally required deductions

Unauthorized salary discussions or payroll manipulation are prohibited.

---

# 9. Performance Management

Performance evaluations are conducted periodically to assess employee performance, achievements, development needs, and organizational contributions.

Evaluation criteria include:

* Job knowledge
* Productivity
* Teamwork
* Communication
* Leadership
* Compliance with company policies

Employees may receive promotions, bonuses, salary adjustments, or development plans based on evaluation outcomes.

---

# 10. Training and Development

The company encourages continuous professional development.

Training opportunities may include:

* Technical skill programs
* Leadership workshops
* Compliance training
* Safety certification
* Customer service development

Employees may be required to complete mandatory training programs assigned by management or HR.

---

# 11. Workplace Safety Policy

Cymbal Landscaping is committed to maintaining a safe and healthy workplace.

Employees must:

* Follow safety procedures
* Use protective equipment where required
* Report hazards immediately
* Participate in safety training
* Comply with environmental regulations

Accidents, injuries, or unsafe conditions must be reported immediately to supervisors or HR.

---

# 12. Information Security and Confidentiality

Employees are responsible for protecting confidential company information, including:

* Employee records
* Customer information
* Financial data
* Proprietary business information
* Strategic documents

Employees must not:

* Share passwords
* Download unauthorized software
* Access unauthorized systems
* Disclose confidential information externally

Violation of confidentiality obligations may result in termination and legal action.

---

# 13. Acceptable Use of Technology

Company devices, systems, email accounts, and internet access are provided for business purposes.

Employees must avoid:

* Illegal downloads
* Offensive content
* Excessive personal use
* Unauthorized software installation
* Cybersecurity violations

The company reserves the right to monitor system usage consistent with applicable laws.

---

# 14. Anti-Harassment Policy

Harassment of any kind is strictly prohibited.

Harassment includes:

* Verbal abuse
* Sexual harassment
* Offensive jokes
* Threats
* Intimidation
* Discriminatory remarks

Employees experiencing or witnessing harassment should report incidents to HR immediately.

All complaints will be investigated confidentially and fairly.

---

# 15. Travel and Expense Reimbursement

Business-related expenses may be reimbursed subject to company approval and documentation requirements.

Employees must:

* Obtain prior travel approval
* Submit receipts
* Follow travel budgets
* Use economical travel arrangements

Expense claims must be submitted within 15 days after travel completion.

---

# 16. Disciplinary Policy

Disciplinary action may be taken for:

* Policy violations
* Poor performance
* Misconduct
* Safety violations
* Unauthorized absences
* Insubordination

Corrective measures may include:

1. Verbal warning
2. Written warning
3. Suspension
4. Termination

The company reserves the right to determine appropriate disciplinary measures.

---

# 17. Resignation and Termination

Employees wishing to resign must provide written notice according to their employment contracts.

During notice periods, employees are expected to:

* Complete assigned responsibilities
* Transfer knowledge
* Return company property

The company may terminate employment due to:

* Performance issues
* Misconduct
* Redundancy
* Operational requirements

Final settlements will be processed according to applicable laws and company policies.

---

# 18. Employee Grievance Policy

Employees may raise concerns related to:

* Workplace behavior
* Compensation
* Management issues
* Discrimination
* Safety concerns

HR will investigate grievances fairly and confidentially.

Retaliation against employees raising legitimate concerns is prohibited.

---

# 19. Data Privacy Policy

Employee personal information shall be collected, stored, and processed securely.

The company will comply with applicable privacy and data protection regulations.

Employees may request access to their personal information through HR procedures.

---

# 20. Policy Amendments

Cymbal Landscaping reserves the right to amend, update, interpret, or revoke policies at any time based on business or legal requirements.

Employees will be informed of significant policy changes through official communication channels.

---

# 21. Employee Acknowledgment

All employees are required to acknowledge that they have received, read, and understood this HR Policy Manual and agree to comply with all company policies and procedures.

---
